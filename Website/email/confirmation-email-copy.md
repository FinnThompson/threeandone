# Confirmation email copy

Paste into Kit: open the form → **Settings** (top toolbar) → **Confirmation Email** tab →
**Edit Email Contents**. Kit inserts the confirm button itself, so leave a blank line
where it's marked below and let Kit's button sit there.

Keep **Auto-confirm new subscribers** UNCHECKED. Ticking it turns double opt-in off
entirely, which hurts deliverability and is legally required in some countries.

---

## Subject line

```
One click and you're in
```

Alternatives if you want something more obviously from the band:

```
Confirm your spot on the Three and One list
```
```
Almost there, Three and One fan
```

---

## Body

```
Thanks for signing up.

Hit the button below to confirm, and we'll keep you posted on new show
dates, merch drops, and whatever else the four of us get up to.

        [ Kit's confirm button goes here ]

If you didn't sign up for this, just ignore this email and nothing happens.

See you at a show,
Three and One
```

---

## Add this line underneath (it directly fights the spam problem)

```
One favor: add threeandoneband@gmail.com to your contacts so our emails
land in your inbox instead of your junk folder.
```

This matters more than it sounds. Asking people to add the sender to their contacts is
one of the few reliable signals a recipient can give their mail provider, and it
compounds: every subscriber who does it improves delivery for everyone after them.

---

## Post-confirmation redirect

Kit lets you send people somewhere after they confirm, instead of showing its
generic thank-you page. Point it at the fan photos page once the site is live:

```
https://threeandoneband.com/fans.html
```

It's a better first impression than a Kit-branded confirmation screen, and it puts
new subscribers straight into the most fun part of the site.
